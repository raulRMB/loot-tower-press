About the game

Loot Tower is a Roguelite Bullet Heaven with ARPG style gear where you battle through hordes of enemies, collect mountains of loot, and increase your power with crazy card combos. Fight your way from floor to floor, ascending higher up the tower to encounter stronger enemies and unlock better gear & cards.

Each run starts from nothing. You draft ability, modifier and stat cards as you climb, combining them into builds that grow stronger with every floor. Every floor of the tower is louder, busier and more rewarding than the one before.

Bring loot back to the shopkeeper to craft, trade and upgrade, then push higher. The farther up you go, the tougher the enemies get. Gear upgrades help you deal with the increased difficulty.

About Ignition Games

Ignition Games is an independent studio in Ontario, Canada, founded in 2026 by two developers. The team comes from Massive Entertainment, Blizzard, Crytek, Offworld and other studios where games are made at huge scale, and started Ignition Games to work the other way around: a small team, short loops, and every decision made by people who play the build every day.

Loot Tower is the studio's first game. It is being built in the open, with devlogs, playtests and honest patch notes.
